## Lesson-pending (descriptive; N below v1 thresholds)

1. **Gold hedge exemption during daily-loss halts:** GLD (and the broader gold_permanent_overlay strategy intent) was closed in the all-positions de-risk on 2026-05-29, despite being positioned as a crisis hedge and showing favorable single-name uPnL (+$215). The crude/energy selloff is precisely the kind of event a gold hedge is meant to weather. The weekly review should assess whether the permanent overlay should be exempt from the daily-loss de-risk trigger — i.e., `halt_after_daily_limit_breach=true` applies to Strategy B (momentum) and Strategy C (if held separately), but not Strategy A when held solely for the overlay intent. This is a policy question, not a calibration error.

2. **Per-name HOLD quality vs wholesale de-risk:** the midday predictions rated CSCO, GOOGL, and UNH as "HOLD-quality / low close probability" (0.70–0.85 confidence) based on single-name theses. All three were nonetheless closed in the all-positions de-risk mandate. This is correct behavior (halt mandate > single-name analysis), but highlights that per-name thesis confidence is distinct from the all-positions halt logic. No recalibration needed; the distinction was explicit in the prediction files.

3. **Crude-driven correlation:** XOM drove both daily-loss breaches (05-26 and 05-29) on the same macro catalyst (US-Iran ceasefire narrative). This suggests either (a) energy exposure sizing should be reduced further, (b) the portfolio's crude sensitivity should be monitored as a separate risk metric (not just the single -10% stop on the name), or (c) the daily-loss-limit cap should be recalibrated for commodity-shock scenarios. Reserved for weekly qualitative review.
