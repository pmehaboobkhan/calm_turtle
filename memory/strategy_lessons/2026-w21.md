## Lesson 1 — Earnings-overlay validation: beat-and-fall is real

**Source:** WMT 2026-05-21 Q1 FY27 print. EPS $0.66 vs $0.65 estimate (beat). Revenue $177.75B vs $174.65B estimate (beat). US comps +4.1% (strong). Stock fell ~8% on the print.

**Observation:** The `holding_earnings_caution_window_days=1` overlay triggered correctly. Pre-close 2026-05-20 exited WMT at $131.23 (−$151.93 realized). Held-through outcome would have been ~−$1,215 MTM on the gap-down. The overlay saved ~$1,063 net of exit cost.

**Why beat-and-fall happens:** High-valuation consumer staples often trade on forward guidance, not backward EPS. When a company beats estimates but provides conservative forward guidance or acknowledges macro headwinds (tariffs, consumer spending normalization), the market prices in the softer outlook. Our strategy sizes WMT as a momentum carry, not an earnings play — so the overlay is correct policy regardless of directional certainty.

**Confidence update:** Increase confidence in the earnings-overlay as a risk-reduction mechanism. Expected value of the overlay is positive even when direction is uncertain.

## Lesson 2 — NVDA earnings-gate: the 1-day caution window works across a multi-session gap

**Source:** NVDA Q1 FY2027 reported 2026-05-20 (strong beat: revenue $81.6B +85% YoY, $80B buyback, dividend raise). NVDA promoted from hold-zone (rank 6) to top-5 entry signal on 2026-05-21. The caution window was deferred through all of 2026-05-21 and 2026-05-22 pre-market/midday/pre-close. EOD 2026-05-22 cleared the gate (2 days elapsed, print far in the rear-view, next earnings 2026-08-26) and approved the entry.

**Observation:** The multi-session deferral worked. The gate prevented entry on 2026-05-21 EOD (1 day post-print, still within the caution window). The 2026-05-22 EOD entry was on a cleaner signal with no active catalyst risk.

**Pattern for future use:** When a strong post-earnings reaction promotes a name from hold-zone to top-5, the entry signal coexists with the highest recency of event-driven volatility. The 1-day window is a minimum; in practice, 2 days post-print may be a stronger default for names that report in after-market sessions (when the AM bar after the print is the first tradeable bar, a 1-day window still catches the immediate post-print session).

## Lesson 3 — CB equity write defect: guard against in-flight fills (MUST-FIX)

**Source:** 2026-05-19 EOD. Intraday routine evaluated CB equity while broker fills were settling — cash debited for pending orders, positions not yet mirrored. Result: a spurious 74% drawdown / HALF state.

**Observation:** The self-correction at EOD confirms the defect is bounded (EOD post-reconcile equity is clean). But the spurious URGENT notification wasted operator attention and temporarily induced false alarm on what was actually a new portfolio peak (+$1,594 day).

**Pattern:** The defect fires on any day when PENDING_BROKER rows exist at the time of an intraday CB equity write (typically market_open). The guard must be: if `paper_sim.pending_broker_count() > 0`, either skip the CB equity write for that routine pass or use `paper_sim.portfolio_equity()` rather than `broker.account_snapshot()`.

**Status:** Tracked. Requires human PR to `lib/portfolio_risk.py` or the intraday routine CB-update call.

## Lesson 4 — Post-earnings gap creates stale-signal ENTRY (needs mechanical guard)

**Source:** WMT ENTRY on 2026-05-21 EOD evaluated on the 2026-05-20 (pre-earnings) daily bar. The signal price ($130.85) was stale; WMT had fallen ~8% on the BMO print that morning. RM correctly rejected; the rejection required judgment ("stale price + defensive posture") rather than a deterministic rule.

**Observation:** The correct outcome (NO_TRADE) was reached, but via an orchestrator judgment call rather than a deterministic signal gate. This is fragile: if a future routine's context is lighter or the soft-daily-loss breach is not active, the same situation might be acted on.

**Pattern for future consideration:** Add a `signal_is_stale` flag: if `signal_bar_date` is T-1 (or older) relative to a known earnings/dividend event in `[signal_bar_date+1, today]`, mark `signal.stale_post_event = true` and route to NO_TRADE mechanically. The earnings overlay's `holding_earnings_caution_window_days` already prevents re-entry during the window; this guard extends it to the signal evaluation layer.

**Status:** Tracked. Requires a human PR to `lib/signals` or signal_consolidator.

## Lesson 5 — Rank-5/6 boundary slot is low-persistence (feature, not bug)

**Source:** UNH / NVDA / AMZN / COST / JNJ all traded in a ~2–4pp band over the week. Rank-5 changed hands: WMT (05-18–05-20) → UNH (05-19–05-22) → NVDA (05-22 EOD).

**Observation:** The rank-5 slot is inherently the lowest-momentum ENTRY signal in Strategy B. It will rotate frequently as names within the ~15–25% 6m return band trade around each other. This is by design: the top-5 strategy is a momentum screen, not a conviction list.

**Implication for sizing:** The current equal-sizing (each position ~6–7% of account, capped at 15%) is appropriate given the rank-5 fragility. Avoid concentrating in rank-5 names when the gap to rank-6 is < 1pp. At < 0.5pp (as with NVDA vs AMZN at 0.42pp), treat the entry as especially low-persistence.

## Lesson 6 — Two consecutive soft daily-loss breaches without a hard halt (portfolio behavior)

**Source:** 2026-05-20 EOD (−1.25%) and 2026-05-21 EOD (−1.77%) both breached the −$500/−0.5% soft daily-loss cap. Hard −2% halt not triggered.

**Observation:** The soft daily-loss limit is a monitoring/defensive-posture trigger, not a hard halt. Both days, no single position was near its −10% stop — the drag was distributed (4–5 names slightly negative). The circuit-breaker stayed FULL throughout.

**Pattern:** Diversified drag (many small negatives) can accumulate across a multi-name momentum book without triggering any individual stop. The soft daily-loss cap catches this before it escalates. Neither day required a position closure, which in retrospect was correct (positions recovered by Friday). However, the close proximity to the hard −2% halt on 2026-05-21 (−1.77% vs −2.0% cap, only 0.23pp margin) is a reminder that the soft cap is early warning, not a safe zone.

**Implication:** When the portfolio is down more than −1.5% with positions near 0 from their entry (not near stops), the pre-close routine should be more aggressive about re-evaluating positions with the weakest near-term outlook. In this case, no individual position warranted a close (all above stops, no catalyst), which was the correct call.
