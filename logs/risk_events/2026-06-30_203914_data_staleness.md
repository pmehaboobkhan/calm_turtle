- **Severity:** ELEVATED (trading-action blocking for this run; not a circuit-breaker event)

- **Mode:** PAPER_TRADING
- **Threshold:** `risk_limits.yaml > data.max_data_staleness_seconds = 60`

## What happened

The EOD routine requires (a) fresh daily bars to evaluate the deterministic
signal universe on **today's close**, and (b) clean close-price marks to process
EXITs/stop-closes. Both inputs were stale beyond the 60s threshold this run:

1. **Daily bars (signal universe):** The default fresh source `yfinance`
   (`BAR_SOURCE=yfinance`) is unreachable in this environment — its curl_cffi
   backend fails TLS through the agent egress proxy (`curl (35) TLS connect
   error`), and does not honor the CA bundle the way `requests`/alpaca-py do
   (a direct curl_cffi call with explicit `verify=/root/.ccr/ca-bundle.crt`
   succeeds, but yfinance does not expose that, and Yahoo also returned 429).
   The fallback `BAR_SOURCE=alpaca` (free IEX) works but its **latest daily bar
   is 2026-06-11 — 13 trading days / 19 calendar days stale** (the documented
   Alpaca free-IEX daily-bar lag). Signals computed on 06-11 bars are NOT a
   valid evaluation of today's close.

2. **Latest quotes (close marks):** `lib.data.get_latest_quote` (Alpaca IEX)
   returned `quote_ts` frozen at 2026-06-30T20:00:00Z (the 16:00 ET close) and
   **~2,210 s (≈37 min) stale** at routine time (20:36–20:39 UTC), 37× over the
   60 s threshold. Spreads were degraded last-of-day IEX prints: CSCO ask=0.0,
   **XOM bid 129.20 / ask 143.28 (≈10% spread)**, GOOGL/NVDA/UNH last==ask with
   bid far below. Not usable as clean execution prices.

   (Contrast: today's pre_close at ~19:37 UTC, during the live session, had
   sub-second fresh quotes — XOM $136.61. By EOD the free IEX feed had frozen
   at the close print and gone stale/degraded.)

## Action taken (capital preservation; CLAUDE.md safety rule #5)

- **NO new ENTRYs** — the signal universe cannot be freshly computed.
- **NO EXIT / stop-close executions** — no trustworthy close price; executing a
  close on a 37-min-stale, ~10%-wide degraded quote risks a materially bad fill
  and is not reliably risk-reducing.
- **HOLD all 7 positions** (CSCO, GOOGL, JNJ, NVDA, SPY, UNH, XOM) overnight.
- Re-evaluate at the next session on fresh data (re-verify XOM and CSCO stop
  status; re-run the deterministic universe).
