## Assessment

- No capital was ever actually at a 74% drawdown. The portfolio is at a fresh
  equity peak ($104,090.72) with 0.0% drawdown.
- The transient `HALF` reading existed only between the cash debit and the
  position mirror-back. It did not cause any erroneous trade: intraday routines
  are monitoring-only and never open/close positions; EOD is the only decision
  point and it is using the correct post-fill equity.
- **Follow-up flagged (lesson-pending):** the intraday CB refresh can record a
  spurious throttle state when broker fills are in-flight (cash debited,
  positions not yet mirrored). The deterministic engine self-corrected at EOD,
  but the intraday `circuit_breaker.json` write during a pending-fill window is
  a known-bad reading. Recommend the intraday routines skip the CB equity write
  (or guard it) when `paper_sim` has rows in `PENDING_BROKER` status. This is an
  observation for the Self-Learning Agent — recorded here and in the journal;
  no config/prompt edit is made by this routine (human-PR territory).
