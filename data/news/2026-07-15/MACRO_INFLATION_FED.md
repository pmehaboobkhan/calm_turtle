This is broad disinflation, not just energy noise. **Implication:** Inflation trajectory broken; fed funds rate at 3.50–3.75% (from June FOMC) is now restrictive, not neutral. Market repricing to near-certain hold on July 28–29 FOMC meeting, with >50% probability of rate cuts later in 2026 or early 2027.

## Risk Scenarios

**Base Case (70% probability):** Inflation stalls 3.0–3.5% for next 2 months. Fed holds July 28–29, November, December. 2027 brings 2–3 cuts (50–75 bps). Equities drift higher, bonds outperform commodities.

**Upside Case (15%):** Inflation re-accelerates above 3.5% (oil persists, shelter stickiness returns). Fed extends hold into 2027, cuts delayed to H2 2027. Bonds sell off, equities consolidate, commodities (oil, gold) remain elevated.

**Downside Case (15%):** Geopolitical oil shock escalates (Strait closure, Iran retaliation spike). Inflation spikes above 4.0% by September. Fed forced to raise rates (1–2 more hikes in 2H 2026). Equities correct 10–15%, bonds volatility increases.
