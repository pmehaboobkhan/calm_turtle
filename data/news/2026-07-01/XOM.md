## Note on Redomiciliation

Redomiciliation is **not** a business event that materially changes shareholder value in the short term. It is a legal/tax optimization mechanism and typically a **positive long-term** benefit (tax efficiency, regulatory flexibility). The neutral classification reflects the lack of immediate operational impact.
