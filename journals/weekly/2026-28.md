Documented with a proposed one-line fix in `prompts/proposed_updates/2026-07-07_confirm_broker_fills_status_normalization_bug.md`. Not patched (lib/ is PR-locked).

The CB Guard-2 basis-gap diagnostic plus a manual cross-check identified this as a feed artifact, not real P&L, before it could affect the zero-close conclusion (which was robust either way — no stop or target was close enough for the gap to matter).
